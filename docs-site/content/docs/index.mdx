**Slipstream** streams your desktop or games from a **Linux host** to another device at that
device's own resolution and refresh rate.

**Clients:** Android (phone and TV), Steam Deck. Any [Moonlight](https://moonlight-stream.org/)
client works when you enable GameStream.

**Model:** private LAN or VPN. No accounts, no cloud relay. Pair once with a PIN, then reconnect on
a pinned identity.

## What you need

- A **Linux** machine with an NVIDIA, AMD, or Intel GPU (software encode is available as a fallback).
- A client: **Android**, **Steam Deck**, or **Moonlight**.
- The same **LAN or VPN**. Do not expose host ports on the public internet.
