# Code of Conduct

Slipstream is a technical project built by people with different backgrounds and experience.
Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to keep discussions useful, respectful, and
focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language,
threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption. Do not publish private information or credentials.

Report a conduct concern privately through the security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or
contact the repository maintainers through GitHub when the issue is not security-sensitive. Reports
are reviewed in confidence and handled according to the circumstances.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may limit participation when these standards are
not met. The goal is a safe project where technical disagreement can remain direct and constructive.
